Please Read This First

Right now, the emotional pain you are experiencing might feel completely unmanageable. It can make you feel as if ending your life is the only way to stop hurting.

You do not have to want to die—you just want the pain to stop. That is understandable, but suicide is a permanent response to a temporary crisis. Emotional states, even the deepest and most painful ones, shift over time.

Give yourself some time. You do not have to make any irreversible decision today.

Things You Can Do Right Now

  1. Put a buffer of time in place: Promise yourself to wait 24 hours, or even just one hour. During this window, focus solely on keeping yourself physically safe.
  2. Remove immediate hazards: Step away from anything dangerous. If you are near a ledge, bridge, or have access to harmful objects, move to a shared or public room, or leave the space.
  3. Try the 5-4-3-2-1 grounding exercise: Look around and name: 5 things you can see, 4 things you can physically touch, 3 things you can hear, 2 things you can smell, and 1 thing you can taste.
  4. Tell someone: Send a short text to someone you trust: "I'm having a really difficult time right now and could use some company or a check-in."

Reasons to Hold On

  • People survive periods like this and go on to live peaceful, fulfilling lives.
  • You haven't seen all the people who will love you, or places that will bring you calm.
  • Help is available, whether through medicine, talk therapy, or peer support groups.
